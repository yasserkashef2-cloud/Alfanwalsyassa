Arabic Connectors and Discourse Markers

An authentic-corpus learning tool covering 408 Arabic connectors and discourse markers, organized by communicative function.

About this corpus — please read before relying on it The Arabic examples, translations and functional explanations below were produced by an AI research assistant (Google Gemini) asked to search real Arabic news archives. They have not been independently verified against the original articles — the tool that produced them could not supply working links to the specific articles it cited, only generic publication homepages, which is why no article links are shown here. Treat every example as a plausible, AI-generated illustration of usage rather than a confirmed quotation, and verify anything you plan to cite elsewhere. Of the 408 connectors in the source vocabulary list, — were never researched at all (shown below as gaps, with a name and rough gloss only, no invented content).
